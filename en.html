Data · “Validation KPIs” Power BI dashboard

Power BI

For every flight, ground agents confirm the time of each task in an app: aircraft on stand, baggage offload, doors closed… But the app lets them backdate a task, for example by validating everything at the end of a shift so that it all looks on time. I built the dashboard that detects, measures and tracks this across every station.

01 The gap

Every validation carries two times: the one the agent claims and the one when they actually confirmed. The gap between them drives the whole dashboard: zero means real-time logging, a large gap means a backdated task.

02 Four quality tiers
  • Excellent≤ 1 min
  • Good≤ 5 min
  • Acceptable≤ 15 min
  • Late> 15 min
03 Two key indicators

On time : the share of validations logged within 5 minutes.

Suspicious : three or more tasks of the same flight confirmed within the same minute, the signature of a batch entry after the fact.

9 pages, 9 questions

  1. SummaryHow healthy is the operation overall?
  2. StationsWhich stations lag behind, and on which tasks?
  3. TasksWhich tasks are backdated most often?
  4. TrendIs it getting better over time?
  5. DetailsWhat is the evidence for one case?
  6. Suspicious flightsWhere does batch backdating happen?
  7. Aircraft typesDoes the aircraft type make a difference?
  8. Flight scorecardWhich flights are the best and the worst?
  9. Head to headHow do two countries or stations compare?

How I built it

  1. The needMeasure task backdating, station by station.
  2. The dataSQL queries on PostgreSQL: joins, DISTINCT ON, COALESCE.
  3. The modelA star schema: a fact table of validations linked to its dimensions.
  4. The measuresIn DAX: gap, quality tier, on-time rate, score out of 100, suspicious flights.
  5. The delivery9 pages filterable by country, station and direction, plus a guide for managers.

01

SurFIT

iOS app for surfers · Personal project · 2026

Private code, shown on request

SurFIT answers the question every surfer asks in the morning: where should I go, and when? For each spot, the app brings together hour-by-hour swell, wind and tide, what other surfers report on site, and the groups to go with.

What makes it smart

  1. Two sources, one forecast

    Swell and tide come from Open-Meteo's marine API, wind from its weather API. The app calls both in parallel, then merges them hour by hour by timestamp, not by position in the list. An hour with missing data is dropped instead of being filled with zeros.

  2. Tide times worked out by the app

    Open-Meteo doesn't give high and low tide times, only the water level hour by hour. The app finds each turning point, then refines it with a parabola through three readings. The error drops from half an hour to a few minutes.

  3. The right day at a glance

    A day picker compares the week at a glance. For today, the app shows the sea right now; for other days, their peak and its time, so Thursday's number is never read as the current sea. A badge sums up the session: great, good, fair, windy or flat, based on height, period and wind.

  4. The beach, live

    Currents, jellyfish, rocks, pollution, crowds: surfers report what they see, and others vote for or against. One vote per person, enforced by the database itself: a second vote is rejected by its primary key.

  5. Surf as a crew, keep your secret spots

    Three kinds of groups: an invisible crew of friends, a publicly listed club, a spot community. Being visible and opening up content are two separate rules: a club can appear in the list without revealing its secret spot or its messages. You join a crew with an 8-character code, and the chat is real-time.

  6. Progress you can't cheat

    Sessions, reports and messages earn XP, with a new level every 100 XP. The app can't write XP itself: only a capped server function grants it, and a trigger blocks any direct change to the profile. Several gains at once show up as a single notification.

Under the hood

  • RLSSecurity lives in the database

    The key built into the app is public by design. Row-level security policies protect every table, a trigger locks XP and roles, and functions are closed to visitors who aren't signed in.

  • 7 → 2Performance audit

    Queries to display the Community screen with 3 groups, after the September 2026 audit. Plus a 30-second cache and real-time channels that no longer stay open.

  • asyncTidy code

    One repository per domain, network calls with async/await, and settings read from an .xcconfig file: switching backends doesn't touch the code.
